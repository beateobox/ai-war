How to Play

Player 1 (Local) Move AD · Jump W · Drop S
Attack F · Skill G
Player 2 (Local) Move ←→ · Jump ↑ · Drop ↓
Attack . or Num1 · Skill / or Num2
Online Move WASD or Arrows · Jump W↑Space
Attack J/F · Skill K/G
Mobile / Touch Move with the joystick: push up to jump (push up again in the air to double jump), pull down to drop. Attack and Skill buttons sit beside it. In local play each player gets a joystick in their own corner. Play in landscape.

Rules

  • Every AI has 100 HP. Hold Attack to keep firing. Skill can only be used once its cooldown is full.
  • You can double jump. Press down to drop through platforms. The small arrow next to each head shows where that player is facing.
  • Press ❚❚ Menu at the bottom of the screen (or Esc) to pause or leave the match at any time.
  • The last player or team standing wins the round.
  • When time runs out: in free-for-all the player with the most HP wins; in team battles the team with more survivors wins, then the team with more total HP.
  • If it is still tied, the round is a draw and every tied side earns a point.
  • Every player must pick a different AI.
